[HEXA AI Agency's audit work turned up a lead-routing automation](https://www.hexaaiagency.com/blog/automation-silent-failures)
that had "been green for weeks." When they counted assigned leads directly in the
CRM, new assignments had stopped 11 days earlier. A form field had been renamed
upstream, so the mapping started writing empty owner values, and the automation
logged that as a successful run every single time, because technically it was one.

## Why this is exactly the ClickUp-Airtable failure mode too

Most no-code automations between ClickUp and Airtable map fields by name: "Status"
in Airtable writes to the field called "Status" in ClickUp. Rename either one, a
client's Airtable base gets a "Status" column relabeled "Project Status," a ClickUp
custom field gets cleaned up during a workspace reorganization, and the mapping
that used to work now points at nothing. The Zap or Make scenario still fires,
still returns success, and still writes nothing anyone will notice until a client
asks why their dashboard hasn't moved in two weeks.

## Why "it ran successfully" doesn't mean what it sounds like

A developer documenting four separate silent failures in their own automations
[put the pattern in one line: "the check reported on something adjacent to the
thing that mattered."](https://dev.to/shipsitself/four-silent-failures-in-my-ai-automations-all-four-ran-green-47lb)
An automation completing without an error only confirms the automation ran. It says
nothing about whether the field it was supposed to update still exists under the
name the automation is looking for.

## What actually catches this before a client does

The fix isn't more alerts on the automation itself, since a renamed field doesn't
throw an error for the automation to alert on. It's periodically checking what
actually landed in the destination against what should be there, [the same
diagnostic this site keeps coming back to for spotting a sync that's about to
break](/blog/signs-your-clickup-airtable-sync-is-about-to-break): a record that
hasn't updated in longer than it should have is the signal, not a red status in a
dashboard that was never going to turn red.
