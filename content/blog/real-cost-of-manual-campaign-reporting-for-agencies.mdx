Every agency reports to its clients, and almost every agency underestimates how much time
that actually takes. It's not one big task - it's dozens of small ones, repeated for every
client, every reporting period, indefinitely.

## An hour per client adds up fast

[One widely cited industry survey found that 59% of agencies spend around an hour per client
on reporting](https://www.wordstream.com/blog/ws/2019/03/18/state-of-the-agency-report). At
ten active clients, that's a full workday every reporting cycle spent pulling numbers into a
document, before any actual analysis or client-facing insight gets added on top.

## Why this task resists automation longer than it should

Reporting feels safe to leave manual because the data pull itself seems simple - log into a
platform, copy some numbers, paste them into a template. It's the repetition across clients,
not the difficulty of any single pull, that makes it expensive. A ten-minute task done for
fifteen clients every week is over two hours nobody planned for.

## What actually needs a person in this process

The data pull, the formatting, and the template assembly don't need judgment - they need to
happen the same way every time. The interpretation - what the numbers mean for this specific
client, what to flag, what to recommend next - does need a person. Automating the first part
and keeping the second is where the actual time savings live.
