ClickUp and Airtable get most of the attention in agency-ops conversations, but neither one
runs alone. A typical agency stack wraps several more tools around them, each handling a
piece of the job ClickUp and Airtable were never meant to cover.

## The stack around ClickUp and Airtable

Communication runs through Slack internally and email externally, kept deliberately separate
from project tracking. Files and assets live in Google Drive or Dropbox, organized by client.
Design work happens in Figma or Adobe Creative Cloud. Proposals and contracts go out through
PandaDoc or DocuSign for e-signatures. Client and sales data often lives in a CRM like
HubSpot, sitting alongside - not inside - whatever's tracking day-to-day project work.

## Why the sprawl happens even though nobody wants it

Most teams would rather run fewer tools. [Roughly 70% of organizations say they'd prefer a
unified platform over a pile of disconnected point
solutions](https://agencypro.app/statistics/agency-technology-statistics), and yet the stack
keeps growing anyway - because each specialized tool does its one job better than an
all-in-one platform does that same job as a feature. Design work belongs in Figma, not a
project management app's file attachments. E-signatures belong in a tool built for
e-signatures.

## Where the actual cost hides

The tool count isn't the real problem. It's what has to move between the tools by hand once
they're all in place. [Knowledge workers already spend an estimated 58% of the day on "work
about work"](https://agencypro.app/statistics/agency-technology-statistics) - the coordination
and status-chasing that surrounds actual output rather than producing it - and every
additional disconnected tool adds another surface for that overhead to grow on.

## What actually needs connecting, not consolidating

The fix isn't collapsing onto a single all-in-one platform and losing what each specialized
tool does well. It's the same principle covered in [why ClickUp and Airtable are the agency
stack nobody talks about replacing](/blog/clickup-airtable-agency-stack-nobody-talks-about-replacing):
a new deal signed in a CRM should be able to create the matching ClickUp project and Airtable
record automatically, without anyone re-entering it three times. See how [tool integration and
data sync](/services) connects a stack like this without replacing any piece of it.
