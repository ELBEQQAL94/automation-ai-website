A new client closes and the real work hasn't even started yet. First comes the checklist:
intake forms, brand assets, platform access, contracts, project setup in every tool the team
uses. None of it is hard. All of it is easy to get partway through and lose track of.

## A process that's the same every time, run from memory

[Typical digital agency onboarding runs seven to fourteen
days](https://www.rocketlane.com/blogs/client-onboarding), largely because it depends on one
person manually working through the same sequence of steps for every new account, with no
consistent system checking whether each step actually happened.

## Where the overhead actually goes

High-performing teams keep the bulk of their time on client-facing delivery; teams that fall
behind end up spending more than half their hours on exactly this kind of administrative
overhead instead. Onboarding is usually the single biggest contributor, because it's the one
process every new client triggers from scratch.

## What a repeatable onboarding actually looks like

The fix isn't a more detailed checklist - most agencies already have one. It's making the
checklist self-tracking: a new client record creates the project automatically, triggers the
intake form, and flags what's still outstanding, instead of relying on someone to remember
where things stand.
